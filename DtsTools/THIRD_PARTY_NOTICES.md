# SOFA converter dependencies

The converter includes Python, NumPy, SciPy, netCDF4, cftime, and their required runtime libraries. Their bundled license files apply to those components.

- Python: https://docs.python.org/3/license.html
- NumPy: https://numpy.org/doc/stable/license.html
- SciPy: https://scipy.org/about/
- netCDF4: https://github.com/Unidata/netcdf4-python
- cftime: https://github.com/Unidata/cftime
- PyInstaller bootloader exception: https://pyinstaller.org/en/stable/license.html

The native validator uses the DTS encoder already installed by DTS Sound Unbound. The DTS encoder is not included in this distribution.
